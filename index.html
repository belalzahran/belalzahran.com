VIN Flasher

Karma Automotive internship, 2023

A Python VIN flasher for Karma's electrification kits, running on a touchscreen Raspberry Pi. It automates legal VIN checks and cut flashing times by 30%, through an OBD-II harness with a Raspberry Pi CAN-bus reader.

Medication Dispenser

Personal hardware project, 2022

A 3D-printed pill dispenser with an ESP32 inside. It rotates the right compartment over a tray on a schedule, lights up when a dose is waiting, and logs every dispense to Firebase so a web app can show history or trigger one remotely.
