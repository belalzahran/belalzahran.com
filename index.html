§ 01   About

I'm drawn to projects that sit at the seams between software and the physical world — a Raspberry Pi bolted to a TV in my bedroom, a desktop app that ships video frames over SSH to a GPU running in the next room, a semantic search index over the 6,236 verses of the Qur'an.

Most of what I build starts as a personal frustration. I don't ship to a market; I ship to myself. A few of the things below started as problems I had at 2 AM and refused to leave alone.
